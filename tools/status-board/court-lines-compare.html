The honest part: the lines still tell the truth

Every measurement is read from the live rule constants, never copied: the arc is drawn at the exact 185 radius zoneOf scores threes with, the corner rails sit on the corner-three strip's inner edge, and the key rectangle IS inPaint's shape, so the three-second rule polices the box you see. Retune a rule and the floor retunes itself. One known seam: value is decided per TILE (by its centre), so a tile the arc slices through belongs wholly to one side. The line is real; the scoring stays square. That is the trade for keeping the squares as they are.

What went away, on purpose

The three coloured difficulty staircases (green/amber/red tier borders) do not exist on a real court, so in this mode they are gone. Difficulty still shows where it always did: on the lit tiles when you line up a move, and on the card itself. If losing the floor-level difficulty read feels wrong in play, the compromise is tinting the TILES faintly by tier with no border lines · say so and I mock it.

Built behind a flag: the live game still shows the old lines until you rule. Nothing about scoring, zones or rules changed · renderer only, per DESIGN.md 9. Say "ship the real lines" and the flag flips with a Settings switch to go back.
