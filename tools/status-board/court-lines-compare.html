The honest part: the lines still tell the truth

Every measurement is read from the live rule constants, never copied: the arc is drawn at the exact 185 radius zoneOf scores threes with, the corner rails sit on the corner-three strip's inner edge, and the key rectangle IS inPaint's shape, so the three-second rule polices the box you see. Retune a rule and the floor retunes itself. Two named seams. FIRST: scoring still uses the old tuned arc (185 units, about 29ft) until Aaron rules on retuning it to the drawn 23ft9in line; measured, the retune moves 20-tiles-worth-3 to 25 per rim, a real balance shift that should be chosen, not slipped in. SECOND: the rim sits 2.2ft OUTSIDE the baseline (game geometry since day one) where a real rim is 5.25ft inside; fixing that means widening the grid, which Aaron pre-authorised ("even if you have to make the court itself larger") and is filed as phase 2, its own build.

What went away, on purpose

The three coloured difficulty staircases (green/amber/red tier borders) do not exist on a real court, so in this mode they are gone. Difficulty still shows where it always did: on the lit tiles when you line up a move, and on the card itself. If losing the floor-level difficulty read feels wrong in play, the compromise is tinting the TILES faintly by tier with no border lines · say so and I mock it.

Built behind a flag: the live game still shows the old lines until you rule. Nothing about scoring, zones or rules changed · renderer only, per DESIGN.md 9. Say "ship the real lines" and the flag flips with a Settings switch to go back.
