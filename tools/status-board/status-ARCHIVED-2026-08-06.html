Heat & ON FIRE — the whole mechanic

LiveV0 item

DESIGN §6 shape: a 12-segment bar (4 quarters × 3) under each scoreboard side. A won card pours 1+tier, the trailing team pours one extra; a miss drops exactly one segment and floors at zero, never wiping. A full bar ignites ON FIRE: every card that team draws is one tier easier and every piece moves one tile further. Any made basket — yours or theirs — or losing the ball ends it. Heat rides the reconnect snapshot; battles are heat-neutral by netcode design. tools/heat-check.mjs, 20 checks, break-proofed.

In plain termsWin cards, catch fire, and being on fire makes you better — never makes your buckets worth more. That was your call on the evidence, and it is the reason a hot streak can't run away with a game.
