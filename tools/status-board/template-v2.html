The source standard one line

A card ships only when its fact has a good-enough source and a recorded human read. Good enough = any Tier 1, or two Tier 2s from different publishers. Nothing merges without audit.py passing, and the audit is a ratchet: old debt passes, new debt fails.

In plain termsBeing well sourced isn't enough on its own — somebody has to have actually opened the page.
