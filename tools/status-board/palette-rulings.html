1 · Surprise Me is iridescent

"Are you able to make pearlescent/iridescent? If so, make that 'Surprise me' in the knowledge level."

Yes. A level that refuses to be one difficulty now refuses to be one colour: the selected pill runs a full-spectrum sheen, and its four ANY TIER chips shimmer with it. It is a copy of the Hall of Fame chip's device (same slide, same 3.2s) so the two retune together, but it runs the whole spectrum where HoF stays anchored to gold, so they never read as the same badge. The stills below catch one moment of it; in the game it moves.

The catch a screenshot could not see

The first cut of the sheen was pinned still: a two-layer keyframe on a one-layer element silently freezes it, and a frozen gradient screenshots identically to a moving one. palette-check.mjs now samples the computed position over 900ms and requires it to have moved. Reduced-motion keeps the spectrum and stops the travel, same as HoF.

2 · Legendary is purple, in all three ladders

Question difficulty, Knowledge level and Pack rarity all said Legendary in gold, and that gold was also Superstar and ON FIRE: one colour, five meanings, the audit's worst finding. All three ladders now say it in #a45cff, picked by measurement: 5.1:1 on the ground and 28.7 dE2000 from its nearest ladder-mate (the audit fails anything under 25).

3 · The pack reveal stops arguing with itself

"Make Epic a positive looking red, and Legendary Purple, Hall of Fame Should Stay Gold."

The before-frame is finding F1 in one look: a gold LEGENDARY chip over four gold SUPERSTAR badges meaning something different. Same squad, after: the chip is purple and gold belongs to the badges.

Epic wore All-Star's exact purple; now it is a rose that reads prize rather than penalty: 13.6 dE2000 from the brick red that means Hard and Wrong, 30.0 from the All-Star purple it used to equal.

Your alternative was already true

"The alternative here is just making Hall of Fame the iridescent color" · the HoF chip has painted an animated six-stop sheen since the reveal shipped, so that option was the shipped truth. It keeps its gold anchor and its sheen; Surprise Me's spectrum is deliberately un-anchored so the two never look like the same badge.

Deliberately left alone, and one thing to know

Everything you said is fine stays as it was: Casual, Easy, Medium, Hard, Common, Rare, team colours, the whole rest of the sheet.

The Daily Five's crowns stay gold on purpose. Its calendar used the same variable for "Legendary difficulty" and "you were there on the day". Repainting the variable would have turned every crown purple, so the two meanings are now two variables, and achievement gold still matches the Heat Check.

One near-miss stands, and it is yours to overrule: the new Legendary purple sits 12.0 dE2000 from All-Star's #b98cff on the reveal. Measured exhaustively: no colour that still reads as purple clears All-Star by more, at the contrast floor. They never label the same kind of thing (chip above, badges below) and the real screenshot reads cleanly, but it is recorded on V0 D28 rather than glossed.
