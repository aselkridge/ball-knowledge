1 · The card covered the action

Fixed
"sometimes the coach covers an action, like a pass, when in the passing drill, and selected another player."Aaron, playing it

I read that as tiles and went hunting for tiles. Measured, no lit tile lands under the card on a 390 phone in any drill sandbox · that reading was simply wrong. What the card sits on is the stagebox, the row that holds CONFIRM ✓ and Pass ✓: 14px of overlap at 1440×760, 1px at 390×844. Below, the card is telling you to hit Confirm while sitting across Confirm.

Two faults, so two fixes

It ate taps. The card is fixed over the canvas, so a finger aimed at the court died on the card. The shell is now pointer-events:none and only its two buttons take a tap.

It hid the button. Pass-through fixes the tap and not the sight, so the card also moves: when the stagebox or a lit tile falls under it, it flips above the HUD and flips back when they clear.

Where it flips to is measured, not chosen. The scoreboard is 174px tall on desktop and 48px on a phone, so one fixed offset cannot clear both · my first attempt was clean at 390 and sat across ORANGE / BLUE at 1440. It now lands at the measured bottom of the HUD.

2 · Off-drill actions are greyed

Fixed
"I want all other actions to be greyed out if they have nothing to do with the current drill... they shouldn't be clickable, or they can be clicked, and nothing happens, and the coach says something like 'whoops, wrong move'."Aaron

You offered two options and this does both: the button greys, and the tap still lands on the Coach's line. A dead control with no explanation reads as a broken game, and in a tutorial that is the worst possible read. If you'd rather they were fully dead, that is now a one-line change rather than a build.

The refusing half already existed. The button-to-action mapping is published by the game and the staged case reuses stagedKind() · the same function the gate itself calls · so the grey and the gate cannot drift apart and start disagreeing.

ON FIRE hands you the fire

Your fighting-game note · "in the tutorial you will start with your meter filled so you can try your special" · is now a rule for every earned state: a drill for a thing you have to earn hands you the earned state at the start. Superstar skills are the next case, and whoever builds them should read this first.

It is also the only route here. Heat deliberately never builds during practice, so a fire drill that did not start lit could never light at all.
