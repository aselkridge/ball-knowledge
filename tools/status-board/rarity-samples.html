The value system, my suggestion

Role = 1 · All-Star = 3 · Superstar = 5. A squad is five cards, so totals run 5 to 25. The bands: Common 7-9 · Rare 10-13 · Epic 14-17 · Legendary 18-21 · Hall of Fame 22-25, with one floor rule: every pack holds at least one All-Star or better (the only hand that cuts is five Role players).

Every combination, enumerated, so the bands are measured rather than felt:

BandEvery five that lands there
Common 7-91A+4R (7) · 2A+3R (9) · 1S+4R (9) — today's "one star, role support" survives as the ceiling of Common
Rare 10-133A+2R (11) · 1S+1A+3R (11) · 4A+1R (13) · 1S+2A+2R (13) · 2S+3R (13)
Epic 14-175A (15) · 1S+3A+1R (15) · 2S+1A+2R (15) · 1S+4A (17) · 2S+2A+1R (17) · 3S+2R (17)
Legendary 18-212S+3A (19) · 3S+1A+1R (19) · 3S+2A (21) · 4S+1R (21)
Hall of Fame 22-254S+1A (23) · 5S (25) — the top of the ladder is four superstars minimum, always

What changes in play: All-Stars finally matter to rarity (today only superstars count), a Legendary can be 2S+3A or 4S+1R (your "any combination" ask), an all-All-Star five reads Epic, and the pack now carries a NUMBER, which is what makes mocks A and B legible. Roll odds stay exactly as shipped (40 · 28 · 20 · 9 · 3): the value system changes what a rarity IS, not how often it hits. The knob if you want superstars to dominate harder or softer: the 5-3-1 spread. 4-2-1 makes tiers closer; 6-3-1 pushes superstars further ahead.
