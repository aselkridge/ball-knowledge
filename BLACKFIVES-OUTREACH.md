## Draft

Subject: Permission request — using "Black Fives Era" correctly in a basketball history game

Hello,

My name is Aaron Selkridge. I'm building a basketball trivia game called Ball Knowledge,
where a player's knowledge of the game is what makes them good at it. It's a personal
project, currently free and played by friends and testers.

I'm writing for two reasons — one to correct something, one to ask permission.

**The correction.** My question bank had a category of early Black basketball history filed
under "Negro Leagues." That's baseball's history, not basketball's, and it was my error. In
researching the fix I found your work, learned the correct term is the Black Fives Era —
1904 to 1950 — and have already begun re-labeling. The questions themselves were accurate:
the Rens' 88-game streak, Bob Douglas, Cumberland Posey's Loendi Big Five, Edwin Bancroft
Henderson, Pop Gates and Dolly King integrating the NBL. I had simply put them under the
wrong heading, and I want to name the era properly.

**The request.** I understand "Black Fives" and "Black Fives Era" are your registered
trademarks, and that you coined them through the research that recovered this history. I'd
like permission to use "Black Fives Era" as the name of this category in the game, and to
credit the Black Fives Foundation on-screen with a link to blackfives.org so players can
go to the source. If you'd prefer I use a different label, I'll use whatever you tell me.

Most people who play this game have never heard of the New York Rens. That's the reason I'd
rather get the name right than quietly work around it.

If there's any interest in going further than permission — reviewing the questions for
accuracy, suggesting what belongs in the set, or anything else — I'd welcome it.

Thank you for the work. It's the reason I knew I had it wrong.

Aaron Selkridge
