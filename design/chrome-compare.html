Clearing the screen

"I don't think any well designed well respected game has text just all over the place for the player... less text without losing UX just like the bolder score for the winning team thing you decided, now THAT was design!!!" Aaron, 08-19

Three things left the game screen and one thing arrived. Nothing that a player needs was removed; what went was text restating what the screen already shows.

Where the removed words went

  • Mode and target ("NBA · FIRST TO 11") are said once at the tip-off and live in the pause menu. They cannot change mid-game, so they were paying rent on every frame to repeat themselves.
  • The quarters counter survives, because it is the one thing up there that actually changes: in quarters mode the strip stays, in first-to-N it is gone rather than dimmed.
  • Which side you are online and the CPU level move to the tip-off, where you are being introduced to the matchup anyway.
  • The selection line is retired outright. A coordinate is how the code talks about the floor, not how a player thinks about it.

Still owed on this screen

  • The board itself (your overhaul note): contact shadows, a real stanchion, thick glass, netting, the floating coordinate letters given a home or retired, and the court lit inside a room instead of sitting on a void. That is the next and biggest pass.
  • The menu header, where the logo currently says the name and then the name is set again underneath it.
  • The colour wash from the beauty pull, which wants the versus screen first.
