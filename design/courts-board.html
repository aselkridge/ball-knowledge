Ball Knowledge · corrected 08-19 · a bug, and a choice

The courts you already own

"why do all my floors look the same… what has happened?!!!"Aaron, 08-19

Because I broke them, hours earlier, and the first version of this board showed the damage without my noticing it. They are fixed. Every court below is the real art, running now.

What happened. The apron I added to ground the floor was drawn as one filled rectangle from outside the court to outside the court, which covers the playing surface too. The sourced texture painted first, then my brown apron painted over all of it, then the tiles went on top at 16 percent. Every court in the game rendered as the same brown deck: hardwood, blacktop, cosmic and underwater, identical. Measured after the fix, the four floors now sample as 209,161,94 maple · 58,56,55 asphalt · 16,16,26 deep space · and their own blue-green, which is the proof the art is reaching the screen again.

The apron is now four bands, a frame, and the court region is never painted over. The hand-drawn plank lines are also limited to the art-less Classic court: on a photographed floor they were my grain drawn over your grain, the same mistake in a smaller size.

The older finding still stands. The default court is Classic, which loads no art at all, so none of this has ever shipped to a player who did not go looking for it.

The choice: which court is the game’s default?

What I would do

  • Make Hardwood · The Cathedral the default. It is the game the art was bought for, and Classic stays in the picker.
  • Then re-judge the board. Two of my four grounding fixes were compensating for the placeholder floor. On real maple, the contact shadows still earn their place; the planks are already retired there, and the jersey plates may be next.
  • And a gate for this class of bug, since suites cannot see it: a check that samples the floor on each court family and fails if two courts render the same colour. Cheap, and it would have caught this in seconds.
