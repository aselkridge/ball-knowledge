PRIOR-ART SEARCH, single question, maximum breadth and DOCUMENTED SCOPE.

THE QUESTION. Who has fused a KNOWLEDGE TEST with a POSITIONAL/SPATIAL GAME —
where what you know determines what your piece can do at a specific position
on a board? The game being built: two-player turn-based basketball strategy
on a tile grid where every scoring attempt is gated by a trivia question and
the tile you shoot from sets the difficulty and value. The moat claim to test:
"trivia as the RESOLUTION MECHANIC for a tactical position has essentially
never shipped."

WHAT COUNTS AS THE SAME FUSION (category i): knowledge gates a SPATIAL action
— this square, this move, this shot — and position changes the stakes of the
knowledge test. WHAT COUNTS AS ADJACENT (category ii): knowledge gates
gameplay actions but with no spatial/positional layer (known example: Quiz
RPG: The World of Mystic Wiz, Colopl 2013 — trivia powers combat, no board).
WHAT IS ONLY SUPERFICIAL (category iii): a board used as a scoreboard or
track (Trivial Pursuit — movement is decoration, position never changes what
knowledge does), or strategy games with trivia bolted on as an optional
minigame. Label every hit i / ii / iii. Do not pad with (iii)s to look
thorough.

SEARCH ALL OF THESE, AND SAY PER LANE WHAT YOU SEARCHED AND FOUND (the
documented scope IS the deliverable):
1. Japanese quiz-game lineage — the deepest vein: Quiz Magic Academy (Konami
   arcade series), Answer x Answer (Sega), Quiz RPG descendants, Magical
   Drop-style quiz hybrids, DS/3DS quiz-strategy titles that never left
   Japan. Japanese-market sources count; name titles even if documentation
   is thin.
2. Educational games, 1980s-2000s — Math Blaster/Carmen Sandiego era through
   DimensionM: did any edutainment title gate TACTICAL POSITION (not just
   progress) on knowledge?
3. Board/tabletop — hybrid trivia-strategy board games (BoardGameGeek's
   trivia + area-control / trivia + tactical crossings; search BGG's
   mechanics taxonomy directly and say what the intersection returns).
4. Quiz-battle video games and arcade — buzz-battle games with positioning
   (Quiz & Dragons, Capcom 1992; Quiz Nanairo Dreams; anything where a quiz
   answer moves a piece on a map).
5. Sports + trivia hybrids specifically — any shipped product mixing sports
   sim/strategy with sports trivia (fantasy-sports trivia games, NBA/EA
   trivia modes inside sims, sports board games with question decks).
6. Modern mobile/Steam — 2015-present: tactics games with quiz resolution,
   trivia roguelikes, "Trivia Quest"-style tactical hybrids. Steam tags +
   store searches count as documentable scope.

FOR EVERY (i) OR (ii) HIT: did it ship, when, how did it do (downloads,
lifespan, shutdown date), and WHAT DID IT GET WRONG per contemporary reviews
