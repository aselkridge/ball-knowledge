BallKnowledge

Your basketball knowledge is your jumpshot.
Turn-based hoops strategy. Chess brain, trivia range.

How you play

Take on the CPU. Go online with a friend on a four letter room code. Or pass one phone back and forth on the couch. Plenty more coming after that.

Leagues and eras

Pick your league, your decade from the 1950s to right now, and how hard the questions come. Then pick your team. NBA and WNBA are open, five more leagues are locked and on the way.

Make it yours

Twelve courts, from a cathedral arena to a cage at golden hour to deep space. Twelve colour themes. Your jersey colours, your squad name, your ball, your soundtrack.

The gameplay

Know the answer and you cross someone. Know the answer and you block the shot. Know the answer and it goes in. String them together and you catch fire.

Built one question at a time

Knowing ball has never won you a game before.

Every question gets checked before it makes the cut. No made up stats, no trick wording, no arguing with the screen because the game had it wrong.

And it goes way past the stuff everybody already knows. The W. The ABA. Playground legends, the Globetrotters, wheelchair ball, and the old Black leagues most games act like never happened. Deep cuts you will not find in another basketball game, and if you know them, you win.
