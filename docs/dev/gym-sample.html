The Gym

seven drills · tap the floor

No score · no clock · easy cardsThe Coach is in
  • Each drill sits where that drill happens. Rebounding under the rim. Shooting at the elbow. Screens at the other elbow. The crossover out on the wing. Defense back in the backcourt. You learn the floor while you pick, and nobody has to explain the map.
  • The court is drawn, not photographed, and now it is drawn RIGHT. The first version of this page invented the lines and Aaron caught it in one look. It has been replaced by docs/play/assets/halfcourt.svg, whose viewBox is the court itself: 50 ft by 47 ft, one unit per tenth of a foot. Corner threes are straight lines 3 ft off the sideline. The arc is 23 ft 9 in from the middle of the rim and meets those lines at their exact tangent, 14.2 ft up. The key is 16 by 19, the free throw circle is 12 across and sits ON the line with its far half dashed, and the restricted area, the backboard and the block are all where they belong. Every number is written into the file. Nothing is eyeballed.
  • The room behind it is a PLACEHOLDER. That is a packed arena, and a gym is the opposite of a packed arena. The real one has to be sourced: an empty practice gym, wide, from about mid-court height, wood floor, banked bleachers or cinderblock, warm practice lighting, nobody in it, 2400px or better.
  • Nothing here is new content. All seven drills run today from coach.js: a Big3 half court, the real engine, frozen clocks, easy cards, and the Coach talking you through it. This is a room to put them in.
  • What is still open: whether the Rulebook lives in here as an eighth station (a clipboard on the sideline) or stays its own door. And whether a drill you have completed shows it, which is the cheapest possible progress feeling.

Medium check, stated before it was drawn. The court and the stations are geometry, so they are built.
