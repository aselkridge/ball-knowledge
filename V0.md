## GATE 1 HAS TWO HALVES NOW — RULED by Aaron, 2026-08-07

> *"is that 1000 wide enough or is it 1000 who was the mvp questions... maybe
> 1000 total questions isn't the request, maybe the request is build as many
> questions as possible, but make sure we have at least 1000 that can cover 20
> people playing 20 times each and getting varying questions."*

**He is right that a raw total is the wrong target, and the arithmetic backs
him.** 1,000 says nothing about what a player experiences. Two banks of 1,000
can feel completely different: one where every card is a Finals MVP question,
and one spread across leagues, eras, difficulties and question shapes.

**The number that matters is per-player exposure, not bank size.** Twenty people
playing twenty games each is not 400 cards, it is twenty separate experiences of
about twenty games. What each of them feels is how often something comes back.
That depends on three things the total does not capture:
- **how many cards a game actually deals** (needs measuring from the real game,
  not estimated),
- **how the pool is SLICED** — a player who picks NBA + 1990s + hard is not
  drawing from 1,000, they are drawing from whatever that intersection holds,
  which today is a small fraction,
- **rotation** — see BUILD.md § 6 · 22ag, filed the same day.

**The likely reframe, for Aaron to rule on:**
1. **Keep 1,000 as a floor, not a target.** It stays the launch gate.
2. **Add a SPREAD requirement**: a minimum per league x era x difficulty cell,
   so the thousand cannot all be one kind of question. The verified-pack gate
   already does exactly this shape at 25 per league x difficulty; this extends
   it to eras and question shape.
3. **Uncap the ceiling.** The real instruction is *build as many as possible*,
   and every question banked beyond the floor improves rotation forever.
4. **Measure the actual experience** before picking numbers: deal 20 simulated
   games per player profile through the real picker and count repeats.
   `gate-check.mjs` already drives the real picker across every league x decade
   x tier, so the harness exists.

### WHAT "PRETTY DIVERSE" MEANS, MEASURED NOT INVENTED

`python3 tools/diversity.py` is the check. Its bars were set by measuring the
318-card pool on 2026-08-07 and asking, per dimension, *is this already fine, or
is this the thing that would make twenty games feel repetitive?* They scale with
the pool, so the question is answerable today rather than only at 1,000.

**Where the pool stands right now: 7 of 8 dimensions pass.**
*Re-measured 2026-08-07 after the era tagging pass and the `all-eras` tag.
Era tagging has gone from the worst dimension to a passing one; history is
still the gap, and it is now a WRITING job rather than a tagging one.*

| | state 2026-08-07 |
|---|---|
| difficulty spread | **PASS** — 40 / 76 / 84 / 68 / 50 across the five tiers, thinnest is 48% of the fattest |
| category breadth | **PASS** — 77 categories |
| subject concentration | **PASS** — Jordan is the biggest at 12 cards, 6% of person-tags |
| breadth of subjects | **PASS** — 95 distinct people across 318 cards |
| top 10 people | **PASS at 34%**, but the bar tightens to 25% at 1,000 |
| league x difficulty | **PASS** — this is the verified-pack gate, already enforced |
| era tagged | **PASS — 14%** of dealable cards untagged, down from 37% (bar: 20%). 42 more are explicitly `all-eras`, which is correct and must be left alone |
| **every era present** | **THIN — 6 eras below the floor**: 1930s, 1950s, 1960s, 1970s, nba-1940s, nba-1950s. *(1890s cleared: 1 card to 16.)* **This is the one remaining gap and Track A · A5 is aimed straight at it.** |

**So diversity is not the problem people would assume.** The bank is not a pile
of MVP questions: difficulty, category and subject are all healthy. **The one
real gap is history** — the pool skews modern. As of 08-07 that is no longer a
tagging problem, it is a supply problem: there are not enough pre-1980 cards to
tag. Writing them is Track A · A5, and the season spine already holds the facts.

### WHAT THAT CHANGES ABOUT THE WORK

### STILL OWED

The real measure is **repeats per player over twenty games**, not a spread
report. That needs the actual picker driven over simulated sessions;
`gate-check.mjs` already drives it across every league x decade x tier, so the
harness exists and the job is to add session simulation. Until it runs, any
claim about what twenty games FEEL like is a guess, and `diversity.py` says so
about itself.

  shortcuts too."* Agreed, and the plumbing is already done: the manifest has a
  `shortcuts` array and `firstScreenDeepLink()` in `game.js` is the one place
  that reads `?go=`, so each new one is a case in that function plus an entry in
  the manifest plus a check in `tools/pwa-check.mjs`.

  shortcut whose url the app ignores is a lie shipped in config. A shortcut and
  the deep link it points at ship together or neither ships. `pwa-check.mjs`
  drives every shortcut url for real, so a broken one fails the harness rather
  than failing on somebody's phone.
  **Android only, and that is Apple's call not ours.** iOS does not support
  manifest shortcuts for home-screen web apps. The iPhone equivalent is adding
  the deep link as its OWN second icon, which works today and is written up for
  players in the install guide (PLACES.md).

- [ ] **DECISION · what the icon is called on a home screen.** `short_name` is
  currently `"Ball Knowledge"`, and iOS truncates the label at roughly twelve
  characters, so it will read **"Ball Knowle…"** under the icon. This is
  branding on the most-seen surface the game has, so it is Aaron's call, not a
  default. Options: **"Ball Knowledge"** (accept the ellipsis), **"Ball IQ"**,
  **"BALL KNOW"**, or **"Hoops IQ"**. One word from Aaron and it is a one-line
  change in `manifest.webmanifest` plus `apple-mobile-web-app-title`.
  *Claude's pick: "Ball IQ" — it survives the truncation, it is what the game is
  about, and the full name still shows in the install sheet.*

- [ ] **DECISION · service worker: yes, no, or later.** Without one, iOS
  Add-to-Home-Screen works completely (it only needs the apple meta tags), and
  Chrome offers "Add to Home screen" from its menu but not the richer install
  prompt. **It was deliberately NOT added today**, because a service worker on a
  GitHub Pages site is the classic way to serve a stale `game.js` forever to
  somebody who already visited, and this is about to go to twenty people whose
  caches nobody can clear. If it lands it needs a versioned cache and a skip-
  waiting path, which is its own job with its own harness. It also overlaps
  **B4**, handling the sleeping server gracefully — an offline shell is one
  honest answer to that — so the sensible time to decide is when B4 is built,
  not now.
