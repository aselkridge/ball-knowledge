  *Cost: ~half a day plus canvas export. Weakness: it is an artefact, not a
  moment. Best paired with A rather than instead of it.*
- [ ] **C · A championship ring in a trophy case.** The most obvious basketball
  reward and the most dangerous to build.
  *Medium: SOURCE THE ART.* A ring is metal, stones, depth and reflection — a

  would be over-promising to pretend otherwise. If Aaron wants this, the ask is

  reuses the fire art already in the repo.
  *Cost: hours. Weakness: it is not a moment and it does not leave the game.*

**All four are in. Suggested order when they come up:** D first (hours, and it is

hold up the other three.
**Do not start any of them before the streak survives a device change.** A

  square scoreboard drawn for the end-of-quarter and final screens — so this is

  `node tools/hud-compare.mjs` shoots the HUD at 390 and prints every control's box.
