## The scope boundary — read this before touching data

**NBA and WNBA only. Complete. Nothing else.**

Aaron, 2026-08-01: *"getting the NBA and WNBA cleaned up and ready is all we need
for now, and that means full data, stats skills everything."*

- **Complete** = full data, stats, roles, current players current, sources tiered,
  answers verified, tags confirmed. Not "exists" — *finished*.
- **Every other league waits.** College, Street, Flags, Overseas, BIG3, G League,
  Early Black Basketball, FIBA 3x3, Wheelchair stay in the tables and stay OUT of
  the packs until their own research lands in a later release.
- **Packs stay switched on**, and serve **only** cards that pass the verified-pack
  gate. The counter tells the truth about how many have earned their place.
